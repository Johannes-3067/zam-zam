How it works

Three steps between you and clear-ish skin by morning.

1

Cleanse & dry

Wash the area and pat it fully dry — patches don't stick to damp skin.

2

Press & forget

Center the dot over the spot and leave it for 6–8 hours, ideally overnight.

3

Peel & reveal

Once it turns white and puffy, peel it off — that's the gunk it pulled out.
